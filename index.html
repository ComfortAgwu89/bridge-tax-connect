The taxes an SME meets, in plain words

Based on the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 and the Nigeria Tax Administration Act 2025, in force since 1 January 2026. The Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS) replaced FIRS.

Company Income Tax

0% or 30%
Who
Registered limited companies.
Rule
0% for small companies (turnover up to ₦100m and fixed assets below ₦250m, not a professional services firm). 30% for all others.
When
Annual return within 6 months of your financial year-end.

Development Levy

4%
Who
Companies above the small company limits.
Rule
One levy on assessable profits that replaces several older charges. Small companies are exempt.
When
With your annual return.

Value Added Tax

7.5%
Who
Businesses selling taxable goods and services.
Rule
Small businesses are relieved from registering, charging and filing VAT unless they opt in by written notice.
When
Monthly, by the 21st of the following month.

PAYE

Per employee
Who
Any employer with staff.
Rule
You deduct income tax from salaries and pay it over. Missing the due date makes you liable for the tax, a penalty and interest.
When
By the 21st of the following month. Your state tax office may set its own schedule, so confirm with it.

Withholding Tax

Varies
Who
Businesses that pay suppliers and contractors.
Rule
You hold back a share of certain payments and pass it to the tax authority.
When
By the 21st of the following month.

Personal Income Tax

0% to 25%
Who
Sole traders and registered business names.
Rule
The first ₦800,000 of annual income is tax-free. Higher bands rise to 25% above ₦50m.
When
Annual self-assessment return.

What a missed filing costs

DefaultPenalty
Failure to file a return, or filing an incorrect one₦100,000 in the first month, then ₦50,000 each further month
Failure to register for tax₦50,000 in the first month, then ₦25,000 each further month
Tax withheld or deducted but not paid by the 21stThe tax itself, a 10% per annum penalty and interest

Amounts are as reported by Nigerian law firms and business press for the 2025 Acts. Sources differ on some thresholds, so confirm current figures with the NRS or your state tax office.

Resources and FAQ

A starter checklist and straight answers

Compliance checklist

0 of 7 done

    If my tax is 0%, do I still have to file?

    Yes. A small company still needs a TIN and must file its annual return. Your records are the proof that you qualify.

    What counts as a small company?

    Turnover of ₦100 million or less, fixed assets below ₦250 million, and not a professional services firm. Both limits must be met together.

    What happens if I grow past the limit?

    The 0% rate and the VAT relief stop applying. Company income tax moves to 30%, the 4% Development Levy applies, and monthly VAT returns begin. Track turnover every month, not at year-end.

    I run a business name, not a company. Does the 0% rate apply?

    No. Business names and sole traders are taxed as individuals under personal income tax, where the first ₦800,000 of income is tax-free.

    Where do I file?

    Federal taxes such as company income tax and VAT go to the Nigeria Revenue Service. PAYE and personal income tax usually go to your state internal revenue service.
